Welcome to Dynamite Faith Church International

We are a Christ-centred community in Atebubu where people of every age and background come together to worship God, grow in faith, build meaningful relationships, and serve others.

Whether you have walked with the Lord for years or are exploring faith for the very first time, there is a place for you here.

  • Worship that is joyful, reverent and Bible-centred
  • Teaching that helps you grow day by day
  • A church family that prays for and cares for one another

Who We Are

Dynamite Faith Church International is a Christ-centred family of believers in Atebubu, Bono East Region. We gather to worship God, to study His Word together, and to care for one another and for our community.
